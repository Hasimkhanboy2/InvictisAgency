Questions Before You Start?

How much does a website cost?+

It depends on the number of pages, complexity, content requirements and integrations. We can scope the project first instead of giving you a random one-size-fits-all price.

How long does a website take to build?+

Simple business websites can move quickly, while larger projects take longer. The timeline depends on scope, content readiness and feedback speed.

Do you work with small businesses?+

Yes. Our focus is local service and home & interior businesses where trust, visibility and enquiries matter.

Can you redesign my existing website?+

Yes. We can first audit the existing site and identify what should change. If a rebuild is the right solution, we can develop the improved version around the business goals.

Will my website work on mobile?+

Yes. The website is built responsively so the experience adapts to mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

Can you integrate WhatsApp?+

Yes. WhatsApp can be integrated into relevant buttons and contact paths so customers can start a conversation more easily.

Will I own my website?+

Ownership and access are agreed as part of the project scope. We will make the arrangement clear before development starts.

Can I update the website myself?+

Yes, depending on how the site is delivered. We can also make the code and content structure understandable enough for practical edits.

Do you provide website maintenance?+

Maintenance can be discussed based on what the website needs after launch.

Can I request changes?+

Yes. The project scope and revision process are agreed before development so you know what is included.

Do you provide SEO?+

Our websites include sensible SEO foundations such as structure, headings, metadata and mobile-friendly implementation. Standalone SEO can be added as the agency offer develops.

Do you manage social media organically?+

Yes. Organic Social Media Marketing can include content strategy, creation, publishing and organic growth direction across relevant social platforms. No paid ads.

Why choose Invictis?+

We use a business-first approach: understand the business, understand the customer, identify the opportunity, then build around it without unnecessary complexity.

What do you need from me to start?+

We need basic information about your business, services, customers, goals, existing digital presence, content or brand assets, and any access required for the agreed work.

What happens after the website launches?+

We can review what can be improved next, including content, organic social, website updates and other practical growth opportunities.
